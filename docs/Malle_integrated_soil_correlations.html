Malle integrated soil correlations

1 Overview

This analysis integrates the February 2026 Malle soil measurements to explore relationships among:

  • MAOM and oPOM carbon concentrations;
  • aggregate stability (mean weight diameter, geometric mean diameter and fractal dimension);
  • oxalate- and DCB-extractable Fe, Al, Mn and Si;
  • Tessier sequential-extraction pools and CEC/exchangeable cations;
  • FTIR peak-area changes after cation extraction; and
  • 2026 radiocarbon measurements (Δ¹⁴C) for Bulk, MAOM, oPOM and fPOM.

Spearman correlations are calculated using pairwise-complete observations. The analysis includes all plots, the basalt-type/application-rate experiment, and the Hühnerberg dose-response experiment. Cross-analysis scatterplots are displayed for pairs with absolute Spearman rho ≥ 0.7. Variables involved in cross-analysis relationships with absolute rho ≥ 0.5 are used in the two subset-specific PCAs.

3 11. Cross-analysis scatterplots

Only relationships between different analytical domains are considered. Scatterplots are shown for cross-analysis pairs with absolute Spearman rho ≥ 0.7. The PCA variable set includes variables involved in at least one cross-analysis relationship with absolute rho ≥ 0.5.

4 12. Subset-specific principal component analyses

Each PCA uses variables selected from the all-plots cross-analysis correlations (absolute rho ≥ 0.5), and is fitted independently within its experiment. Missing values are median-imputed within each subset; variables with fewer than three observed values or no variance are removed before fitting.
